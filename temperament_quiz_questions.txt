# 4 temperament questions 

High E + Low N → Sanguine
High E + High N → Choleric
Low E + Low N → Phlegmatic
Low E + High N → Melancholic

no likert , yes no maybe format 


1. Do you often feel a craving for new experiences, to shake things up, or to feel a thrill?
2. Do you often feel that you need friends who can understand you, encourage you, or sympathize with you?
3. Do you consider yourself a carefree person?
4. Is it very hard for you to give up on your intentions?
5. Do you think things over slowly and prefer to wait before acting?
6. Do you always keep your promises, even when it's inconvenient for you?
7. Do you often have mood swings — highs and lows?
8. Do you usually speak and act quickly?
9. Have you ever felt unhappy without any real reason for it?
10. Is it true that you could dare to do anything on a dare?
11. Do you feel shy when you want to get to know someone of the opposite sex that you like?
12. Does it ever happen that when you get angry, you lose your temper?
13. Does it often happen that you act without thinking, on impulse?
14. Do you often worry about things you feel you shouldn't have done or said?
15. Do you prefer reading books over meeting people?
16. Is it true that you are easily offended?
17. Do you like going out and being in company often?
18. Do you sometimes have thoughts you would rather not share with others?
19. Is it true that sometimes you are so full of energy that everything goes great, but other times you feel exhausted?
20. Do you try to keep your social circle small, limited to your closest friends?
21. Do you daydream a lot?
22. When someone shouts at you, do you shout back?
23. Do you often feel guilty about something?
24. Do you consider all your habits to be good ones?
25. Are you sometimes able to let yourself go and have a great time in a lively group?
26. Would you say your nerves are often stretched to the limit?
27. Are you known as a lively and cheerful person?
28. After something is done, do you often think back and feel you could have done it better?
29. Do you feel at ease when you're in a large group?
30. Do you ever pass on rumors?
31. Does it happen that you can't sleep because different thoughts keep running through your head?
32. If you want to find something out, do you prefer to look it up in a book rather than ask someone?
33. Do you ever have a racing heartbeat?
34. Do you enjoy work that requires concentration?
35. Do you ever have fits of trembling?
36. Do you always tell the truth?
37. Do you find it unpleasant to be in a group where people tease each other?
38. Are you an irritable person?
39. Do you enjoy work that requires quick action?
40. Is it true that you are often troubled by thoughts about unpleasant things and horrors that could have happened, even though everything turned out fine?
41. Is it true that you are slow in your movements and somewhat unhurried?
42. Are you ever late for work or for a meeting with someone?
43. Do you often have nightmares?
44. Is it true that you love to talk so much that you never miss a chance to chat with a new person?
45. Do you suffer from any aches or pains?
46. Would you feel upset if you couldn't see your friends for a long time?
47. Would you call yourself a nervous person?
48. Are there people among your acquaintances that you clearly dislike?
49. Would you call yourself a self-confident person?
50. Are you easily hurt by criticism of your faults or your work?
51. Is it hard for you to genuinely enjoy events where a lot of people are present?
52. Are you bothered by a feeling that you are somehow worse than others?
53. Could you liven up a dull gathering?
54. Does it happen that you talk about things you know nothing about?
55. Do you worry about your health?
56. Do you like to play pranks on others?
57. Do you suffer from insomnia?
