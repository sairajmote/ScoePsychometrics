Examination Overview

Assessment Structure

The ScoePsychometrics assessment is a comprehensive evaluation designed to provide a high-fidelity psychological and cognitive profile. The examination covers eight core modules:

  • MBTI (Myers-Briggs): Analysis of psychological preferences in how you perceive the world and make decisions.
  • Big Five Personality: Measures Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism.
  • Enneagram: Exploration of nine personality archetypes and core motivational patterns.
  • Brain Dominance: Assessment of left-brain versus right-brain cognitive preferences.
  • Temperament (Eysenck): Classifies your core temperament as Sanguine, Choleric, Phlegmatic, or Melancholic.
  • Multiple Intelligence: Identifies your dominant intelligences across nine categories.
  • Cognitive Abilities: Evaluates forgetfulness, distractibility, false triggering, and logical reasoning.

Examination Guidelines

Candidates are required to adhere to the following protocols to ensure the integrity and accuracy of the assessment:

  • Environment: Candidates must ensure a controlled, quiet environment free from distractions.
  • Integrity: The use of external aids, unauthorized resources, or collaborative assistance is strictly prohibited.
  • Completion: All sections must be completed within the single allocated session to maintain psychological consistency.
  • Honesty: Answer based on how you actually are, not how you wish to be.
