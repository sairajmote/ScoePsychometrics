Examination Overview

Assessment Structure

The ScoePsychometrics assessment is a comprehensive evaluation designed to provide a high-fidelity psychological and cognitive profile. The examination is structured into five core modules:

  • MBTI (Myers-Briggs): Analysis of psychological preferences in how you perceive the world and make decisions.
  • Temperament (Eysenck): Measurement of Extraversion and Neuroticism dimensions to classify your core temperament as Sanguine, Choleric, Phlegmatic, or Melancholic.

Examination Guidelines

Candidates are required to adhere to the following protocols to ensure the integrity and accuracy of the assessment:

  • Duration: The total allocated time for the examination is 120 minutes.
  • Environment: Candidates must ensure a controlled, quiet environment free from distractions.
  • Integrity: The use of external aids, unauthorized resources, or collaborative assistance is strictly prohibited.
  • Completion: All sections must be completed within the single allocated session to maintain psychological consistency.
